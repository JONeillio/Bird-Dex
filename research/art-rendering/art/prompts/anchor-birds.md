# Anchor bird generation briefs

These are ready-to-use briefs, not generated image files. Use the image generator's transparent-background option. Once an anchor is accepted, include the actual accepted image as a style reference for later birds; a written prompt alone does not guarantee cross-batch consistency.

## Common instruction

> Create an original retro handheld pixel-art game portrait of the specified real bird. Show exactly one complete bird in a calm right-facing side/three-quarter pose with its eye visible. Preserve the species' natural proportions and diagnostic colours. Use restrained outlines, deliberate pixel clusters and a small clear set of shade steps. Transparent background; no scenery, words, interface, card border, badge, glow, cast shadow or extra animals. Keep bill, tail and feet comfortably within a square framing, suitable for a later 64×64 native-grid export. Treat the result as a production draft for review, not proof that the generator returns exact grid dimensions. Match the BirdQuest concept's warm, charming game-art character while keeping the bird recognisable.

## European robin

Asset: `bird.european-robin.portrait.adult.v1`.

Represent an adult European robin, *Erithacus rubecula*, UK reference. Small round-bodied songbird; orange-red face and breast, brown upperparts and pale belly; slim bill. Preserve this identity rather than drawing an American robin. A simple standing pose, no perch/background props. Apply the common instruction.

Primary identity reference: https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/robin

## Common kingfisher

Asset: `bird.common-kingfisher.portrait.adult.v1`.

Represent an adult common kingfisher, *Alcedo atthis*, UK reference. Compact body, short tail, long straight bill, recognisable blue upperparts and warm orange underside. Choose one documented adult sex/plumage depiction during reference review, record it in the manifest and make the bill markings consistent with that depiction. Avoid a hybrid of different variants. Bird only, no reed or branch. Apply the common instruction.

Primary identity reference: https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/kingfisher

## Grey heron

Asset: `bird.grey-heron.portrait.adult.v1`.

Represent an adult grey heron, *Ardea cinerea*, UK reference. Tall long-legged bird with a long bill and neck; preserve the grey/white/black pattern and adult head markings from the reference. A natural standing posture with the whole bird, including legs, inside the frame. Keep the heron's tall silhouette rather than compressing it into a round songbird shape. No water/scenery. Apply the common instruction.

Primary identity references: https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/grey-heron and https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Gray_Heron/id

## Review and provenance

Keep the original generation and exact prompt, date/tool when actually known, variant choice, reference URLs and any edits. Check anatomy, silhouette, pixel clusters, alpha, safe margins and in-card appearance before approval.
