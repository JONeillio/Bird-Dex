# Dot Lau · Keeper of the Dex
Dot is a semi-retired British Chinese signwriter from Deptford. She draws a bird in a spiral sketchbook and uses the drawing to show the player what to look for. Her warmth comes through a sideways glance, a small smile and occasionally a joke about her drawings.

## Game art
Use the eight 96×96 single-pose PNGs and the 32×32 head icon at integer scales where possible. All have transparent backgrounds. The current delighted pose is the quiet, closed-mouth smile. The eight states and their intended events are listed in asset_manifest.json.

The 96×96 GIF animates her pencil. Engines that prefer sprites can read the two 96×96 cells of dot-lau-sketching-cels-96.png from left to right. Play cell 0 for 700 ms, 1 for 260 ms, 0 for 180 ms, then 1 for 540 ms. Use the sketching still with reduced motion.

## Art history
The source images retain their original full resolution and composition. Some exports were mirrored to face the dialogue area. Files containing archive are concept work, including Dot's first casting appearance, the early handheld device and the superseded open-mouth laugh. They are available for reference.
