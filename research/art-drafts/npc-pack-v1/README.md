# Bird-Dex NPC Asset Pack v1

Twelve original NPC portraits and their encounter concepts, developed on 8 November 2026.

## Contents

This archive has one flat layout. It contains 12 named PNG portraits, this README and encounters.json. All PNGs retain their original 1254 × 1254 size and transparent backgrounds. Portraits were generated for Bird-Dex with the built-in image generation tool.

The PNGs are dialogue portraits. Map sprites, expression variants, scenery, props as separate images, audio, reward icons and implemented game screens remain future production work. Rewards described below are narrative concepts.

## Setting and placement

The starting area is Erith, in the London Borough of Bexley. Bursted Woods is the proposed opening chapter. Its mix of woodland, open grass, nearby railway and bus-served roads supports a suburban southeast London atmosphere. Erith Pier provides a later Thames chapter.

Val, Alfie and Leanne already had these regional placements in the conversation. The earlier characters began in a shared fictional wood. This pack proposes local settings for Nell, Kit, Imani, Bea, Dev, Moss and Ruth. June and Otis still need a mapping decision.

All characters and businesses are fictional. Places assigned to characters are game-design suggestions. Exact coordinates and production map markers are unset. The hide, bakery yard, brook, bridge, bench and Waggy Bridge name are fictional scene features. The local RSPB page lists no facilities at Bursted Woods. Any real-world route or accessibility details need checking when location-based play is implemented.

## Suggested encounter presentation

Tap a character on the map, move into a closer view of their surroundings, then reveal their portrait and opening speech. Their scene, ambient sounds and gesture establish what they are doing. Small observation activities and repeat visits reveal more of the story.

Possible rewards include journal objects, illustrations, recordings, a cosmetic palette, a friendly champion pin and lasting changes to the scene. Val is the proposed first contact. Her short encounter introduces looking and listening.

## Nell — The dawn-chorus recordist

**Portrait** — nell.png

**Appearance** — Young Black British field recordist with curls, ochre headphones, teal rain shell and handheld recorder.

**Suggested setting** — A path just inside Bursted Woods. Suggested home for the original woodland encounter. The exact path and seating are fictional.

**When to meet** — Early morning

**Opening line**

> There. Hear that little phrase? Wait for him to sing it again.

**Story**

Nell spends her mornings collecting the sounds most people walk straight past. She is delighted when a familiar call emerges from the noise, and wants you to hear it for yourself. Dev later recognises a phrase in one of her recordings.

**When the camera moves in**

Move in from the path to Nell beside the trees. She lifts one headphone cup, pauses her recorder and looks towards the canopy. Road noise fades enough to make the bird phrase clear.

**Encounter idea**

Listen to a short call, wait for its repetition, then select the matching phrase. Later visits add another recording or a conversation with Dev.

**Keepsake or reward** — A labelled recording for the field journal. Replay the encounter audio from the journal.

**Return visit line**

> I kept the bit where we both heard it. You can hear you smiling.

**Connections** — dev.

**Development note** — Original portrait and opening line. Keepsake and return visit developed for this pack.

## Kit — The self-appointed wren expert

**Portrait** — kit.png

**Appearance** — Freckled red-haired child in a blue hat and mustard coat, with oversized binoculars and a wren on his hat.

**Suggested setting** — The scrubby edge of Bursted Woods. Suggested setting for his existing wren encounter. The precise clearing is fictional.

**When to meet** — Daytime

**Opening line**

> If you see it, just point. Last time someone shouted, it flew off.

**Story**

Kit has been standing still for twenty minutes and is certain that patience will make him the best wren spotter in the wood. He has yet to notice that the bird is sitting on his hat.

**When the camera moves in**

Move from the woodland edge into a close view of Kit holding a finger to his lips. A tiny movement above his hat gives the player the answer before Kit sees it.

**Encounter idea**

Tap the wren quietly. Kit follows your gaze, looks up too late and has to accept your account of where it was.

**Keepsake or reward** — A hand-drawn wren sticker. A cosmetic sticker for a journal page.

**Return visit line**

> It came back. Different hat today. I'm testing a theory.

**Development note** — Original portrait and opening line. Keepsake and return visit developed for this pack.

**Suggested setting** — A fictional hide or sheltered sketching bench in the Bursted Woods chapter. A game scene. No real hide at Bursted Woods has been established. Local RSPB information lists no facilities.

**Opening line**

> Same bird in both drawings. Reckon you can name it from the way it climbs?

**Story**

Imani sketches behaviour before she worries about perfect feathers. Her two nuthatch drawings show how posture can reveal a bird, even when you only catch a glimpse. She lets the player cover one drawing and work from the other.

**Encounter idea**

Identify the nuthatch from its headfirst climbing pose. Imani reveals both drawings and talks through the movement she noticed.

**Keepsake or reward** — A copy of her nuthatch study. An illustrated behaviour note in the field journal.

**Return visit line**

> That one's yours to finish. I've left you the interesting bit.

**Connections** — bea.

**Keepsake or reward** — A bakery order slip with the bird rhythm on the back. A two-sided journal item linking Dev and Bea.

**Return visit line**

**Connections** — nell, bea.

## Using this pack in the app

Keep the PNG files and encounters.json together when importing, or update image paths to match the repo. Each character has a stable lowercase id. portrait.file resolves relative to encounters.json. character_order preserves the order in which the cast was created.

Read opening_line for the first speech box. The story, scene, encounter and reward fields are design notes for integration. Build the required screens and state using the app's existing conventions. Repeat-visit lines belong after the described encounter has happened.

Each portrait includes its dimensions, transparency flag and SHA-256 checksum in encounters.json. Preserve the aspect ratio and alpha channel. Use pixelated rendering or nearest-neighbour scaling where supported. The images have a pixel-art appearance with some textured edges, so they may need a later art pass if the app adopts a strict low-resolution sprite grid.

The original three screenshot references are visual inspiration. This pack contains the twelve new standalone portraits generated in this conversation.
